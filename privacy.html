Privacy Policy — ytauto

Last updated: November 7, 2026

ytauto is a personal application operated by a single individual to upload videos to, and read statistics for, YouTube channels that the same individual owns. It is not available to the public.

Data accessed

Through the YouTube API Services, ytauto accesses only the operator's own channels: it uploads videos, reads channel and video metadata, and reads YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time, subscribers).

How data is used and stored

Data is used solely to publish the operator's videos and to produce private performance reports for the operator. OAuth tokens and reports are stored on the operator's private server and are not shared with, sold to, or disclosed to any third party.

Third-party services

ytauto uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account security settings. Stored data is deleted when access is revoked or on request.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the support email shown on this app's Google sign-in consent screen.
